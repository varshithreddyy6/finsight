# IronPeak Energy Annual Report FY2021
## Executive Summary
IronPeak Energy reported revenue of $4100 million, gross profit of $1230 million and operating income of $492 million.
## Revenue and Profitability
Revenue was $4100 million. Gross margin was 30.0% and operating margin was 12.0%. Net income was $260 million.
## Cash Flow and Balance Sheet
Free cash flow was $330 million. Cash was $310 million, current assets $920 million and current liabilities $720 million. Debt was $2600 million and equity $2100 million. EPS was $1.82.
## Outlook and Risk Factors
Management noted commodity-price volatility and cyclical demand. Leverage remains meaningful, though liquidity is monitored closely and the outlook depends on energy markets.
