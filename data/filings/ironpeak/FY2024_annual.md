# IronPeak Energy Annual Report FY2024
## Executive Summary
IronPeak Energy reported revenue of $4380 million, gross profit of $1260 million and operating income of $438 million.
## Revenue and Profitability
Revenue was $4380 million. Gross margin was 28.8% and operating margin was 10.0%. Net income was $190 million.
## Cash Flow and Balance Sheet
Free cash flow was $245 million. Cash was $275 million, current assets $875 million and current liabilities $910 million. Debt was $2820 million and equity $2550 million. EPS was $1.28.
## Outlook and Risk Factors
Management noted commodity-price volatility and cyclical demand. Leverage remains meaningful, though liquidity is monitored closely and the outlook depends on energy markets.
