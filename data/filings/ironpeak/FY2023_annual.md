# IronPeak Energy Annual Report FY2023
## Executive Summary
IronPeak Energy reported revenue of $4520 million, gross profit of $1356 million and operating income of $542 million.
## Revenue and Profitability
Revenue was $4520 million. Gross margin was 30.0% and operating margin was 12.0%. Net income was $301 million.
## Cash Flow and Balance Sheet
Free cash flow was $370 million. Cash was $360 million, current assets $1010 million and current liabilities $850 million. Debt was $2710 million and equity $2640 million. EPS was $2.01.
## Outlook and Risk Factors
Management noted commodity-price volatility and cyclical demand. Leverage remains meaningful, though liquidity is monitored closely and the outlook depends on energy markets.
