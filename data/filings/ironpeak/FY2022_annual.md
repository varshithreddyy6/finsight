# IronPeak Energy Annual Report FY2022
## Executive Summary
IronPeak Energy reported revenue of $4870 million, gross profit of $1656 million and operating income of $731 million.
## Revenue and Profitability
Revenue was $4870 million. Gross margin was 34.0% and operating margin was 15.0%. Net income was $480 million.
## Cash Flow and Balance Sheet
Free cash flow was $570 million. Cash was $450 million, current assets $1150 million and current liabilities $780 million. Debt was $2440 million and equity $2520 million. EPS was $3.12.
## Outlook and Risk Factors
Management noted commodity-price volatility and cyclical demand. Leverage remains meaningful, though liquidity is monitored closely and the outlook depends on energy markets.
