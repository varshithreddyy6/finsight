# IronPeak Energy Annual Report FY2025
## Executive Summary
IronPeak Energy reported revenue of $4760 million, gross profit of $1476 million and operating income of $619 million.
## Revenue and Profitability
Revenue was $4760 million. Gross margin was 31.0% and operating margin was 13.0%. Net income was $364 million.
## Cash Flow and Balance Sheet
Free cash flow was $405 million. Cash was $342 million, current assets $1040 million and current liabilities $890 million. Debt was $2740 million and equity $2670 million. EPS was $2.36.
## Outlook and Risk Factors
Management noted commodity-price volatility and cyclical demand. Leverage remains meaningful, though liquidity is monitored closely and the outlook depends on energy markets.
