# Aurelius Systems Annual Report FY2024
## Executive Summary
Aurelius Systems reported revenue of $1214 million, gross profit of $579 million and operating income of $278 million.
## Revenue and Profitability
Revenue was $1214 million. Gross margin was 47.7% and operating margin was 22.9%. Net income was $210 million.
## Cash Flow and Balance Sheet
Free cash flow was $244 million. Cash was $421 million, current assets $635 million and current liabilities $250 million. Debt was $150 million and equity $1098 million. EPS was $2.63.
## Outlook and Risk Factors
Management expects constructive demand and continued disciplined investment. No material weaknesses were identified in internal controls. Liquidity remains strong and the outlook is positive.
