# Aurelius Systems Annual Report FY2021
## Executive Summary
Aurelius Systems reported revenue of $842 million, gross profit of $347 million and operating income of $153 million.
## Revenue and Profitability
Revenue was $842 million. Gross margin was 41.2% and operating margin was 18.2%. Net income was $112 million.
## Cash Flow and Balance Sheet
Free cash flow was $128 million. Cash was $280 million, current assets $430 million and current liabilities $210 million. Debt was $190 million and equity $780 million. EPS was $1.42.
## Outlook and Risk Factors
Management expects constructive demand and continued disciplined investment. No material weaknesses were identified in internal controls. Liquidity remains strong and the outlook is positive.
