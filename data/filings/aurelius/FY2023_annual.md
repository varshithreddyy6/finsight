# Aurelius Systems Annual Report FY2023
## Executive Summary
Aurelius Systems reported revenue of $1088 million, gross profit of $501 million and operating income of $235 million.
## Revenue and Profitability
Revenue was $1088 million. Gross margin was 46.0% and operating margin was 21.6%. Net income was $174 million.
## Cash Flow and Balance Sheet
Free cash flow was $198 million. Cash was $365 million, current assets $550 million and current liabilities $238 million. Debt was $168 million and equity $966 million. EPS was $2.18.
## Outlook and Risk Factors
Management expects constructive demand and continued disciplined investment. No material weaknesses were identified in internal controls. Liquidity remains strong and the outlook is positive.
