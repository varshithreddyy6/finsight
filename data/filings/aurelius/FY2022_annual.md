# Aurelius Systems Annual Report FY2022
## Executive Summary
Aurelius Systems reported revenue of $961 million, gross profit of $418 million and operating income of $190 million.
## Revenue and Profitability
Revenue was $961 million. Gross margin was 43.5% and operating margin was 19.8%. Net income was $139 million.
## Cash Flow and Balance Sheet
Free cash flow was $157 million. Cash was $318 million, current assets $486 million and current liabilities $224 million. Debt was $180 million and equity $861 million. EPS was $1.75.
## Outlook and Risk Factors
Management expects constructive demand and continued disciplined investment. No material weaknesses were identified in internal controls. Liquidity remains strong and the outlook is positive.
