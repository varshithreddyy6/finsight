# Aurelius Systems Annual Report FY2025
## Executive Summary
Aurelius Systems reported revenue of $1340 million, gross profit of $627 million and operating income of $318 million.
## Revenue and Profitability
Revenue was $1340 million. Gross margin was 46.8% and operating margin was 23.7%. Net income was $252 million.
## Cash Flow and Balance Sheet
Free cash flow was $286 million. Cash was $498 million, current assets $745 million and current liabilities $260 million. Debt was $132 million and equity $1240 million. EPS was $3.14.
## Outlook and Risk Factors
Management expects constructive demand and continued disciplined investment. No material weaknesses were identified in internal controls. Liquidity remains strong and the outlook is positive.
